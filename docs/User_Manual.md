# PEN-Plus Management System: User Manual

Welcome to the PEN-Plus Management System. This manual will guide you through the primary features and workflows available in the platform.

## 1. System Overview

The PEN-Plus system is designed to seamlessly manage health data across multiple levels of government and healthcare facilities. It provides dedicated dashboards depending on your administrative level.

## 2. User Roles

Your access and view within the system depend on your assigned role:
- **National / PI:** Full visibility across all regions and facilities. Access to aggregate national reports and indicators.
- **Regional:** Visibility restricted to facilities within the assigned region.
- **District:** Visibility restricted to facilities within the assigned district.
- **Facility (Clinic/Hospital):** Direct data entry, patient management, and facility-specific dashboards.
- **Mentor:** Specialized role for training and evaluating facility doctors.

## 3. Dashboards

The system features customized views based on user needs:
- **Hospital Admin Dashboard:** The central hub for facility-level administrators. Shows high-level metrics, revenue, and overall patient counts.
- **Medical Dashboard:** Tailored for medical officers to track operational metrics, doctor availability, and ward statuses.
- **Clinic Dashboard:** Focused on outpatient and specific clinic tracking.

### Navigation
Use the **Sidebar** on the left to seamlessly navigate between your available dashboards. The **Navbar** at the top provides quick actions, profile settings, and date-range filters.

## 4. Patient Registry (Coming Soon)

The upcoming Patient Registry module will allow you to:
- Register new patients
- View historical medical data
- Track PEN-Plus specific indicators and treatment outcomes.

All data entered is strictly audited, ensuring accountability and preventing accidental deletion (via safe soft-deletes).
