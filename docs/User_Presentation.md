# Presentation: PEN-Plus Management System

---

## Slide 1: Introduction
**PEN-Plus Management System**
*Empowering Healthcare through Data-Driven Management*

- Designed for the PEN-Plus initiative.
- Built to scale from clinical studies to full Ministry of Health integration.

---

## Slide 2: The Challenge
- Managing patient data across multiple tiers of government.
- Ensuring accountability and data integrity.
- Providing tailored insights to different stakeholders (PIs, Mentors, Facility Staff).

---

## Slide 3: Our Solution
A robust, hierarchical web application tailored for medical administration.

**Key Pillars:**
1. **Role-Based Access:** Unique views for National, Regional, District, and Facility users.
2. **Auditable Trails:** Every action is tracked (Who, When, What). No data is ever permanently lost.
3. **Beautiful UI:** A premium, modular interface (Apollo Template) ensuring user adoption and ease-of-use.

---

## Slide 4: Key Features Today
- **Custom Hierarchical Roles:** From Principal Investigators to Mentors.
- **Modular Dashboards:** Hospital Admin, Medical, and Clinic dashboards ready for data injection.
- **Bulletproof Architecture:** Django & PostgreSQL foundation.

---

## Slide 5: The Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Complete):** Core infrastructure, UI templating, and User/Role management.
- **Phase 2 (Next):** Patient Registry implementation.
- **Phase 3:** Reports and Indicators dashboarding for upper-level management.
